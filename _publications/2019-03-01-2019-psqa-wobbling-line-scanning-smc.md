Purpose: This study aimed to report the initial experience of patient-specific quality assurance (pQA) for the wobbling and line-scanning proton therapy at Samsung Medical Center. Materials and Methods: The pQA results of 89 wobbling treatments with 227 fields and 44 line-scanning treatments with 118 fields were analyzed from December 2015 to June 2016. For the wobbling method, proton range and spread-out Bragg peak (SOBP) width were verified. For the line-scanning method, output and two-dimensional dose distribution at multiple depths were verified by gamma analysis with a 3%/3 mm criterion. Results: Wobbling treatments showed range differences of -0.44 mm for small radius and 0.1 mm for middle radius cases. Line-scanning output remained within plus or minus 3%, and gamma passing rates were over 95% with the 3%/3 mm criterion for all depths. Conclusions: Both treatment methods demonstrated acceptable performance metrics, with wobbling parameters and line-scanning dose distributions showing strong agreement between measured and planned values.
