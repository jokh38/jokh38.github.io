Purpose: to describe the proton therapy system at Samsung Medical Center (SMC-PTS), including the proton beam generator, irradiation system, patient positioning system, patient position verification system, respiratory gating system, and operating and safety control system, and to review the current status of the SMC-PTS. The proton beam generator, including the cyclotron and the energy selection system, can lower the energy of protons down to 70 MeV from the maximum 230 MeV. The multi-purpose nozzle can deliver both wobbling proton beam and active scanning proton beam, and a multi-leaf collimator has been installed downstream of the nozzle. The dedicated scanning nozzle uses a helium gas filled pipe minimizing unnecessary interactions with the air. RayStation is used as the treatment planning system and MOSAIQ handles data management. The SMC-PTS, located in Seoul, Korea, was scheduled to begin treating cancer patients in 2015.
