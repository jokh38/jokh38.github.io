Dose modeling of proton pencil beam scanning (PBS) in a commercial treatment planning system requires absolute integral depth dose (IDD) curves and spot profiles for all energies. At the Korea National Cancer Center (KNCC), 133 IDDs spanning an energy range of 99.3-226.9 MeV and 2128 x, y spot profiles were measured from February to July 2014, corresponding to all available energy degrader steps. These measurements sufficed for dose modeling and represent the first proton pencil beam dose modeling data set produced in Korea, providing a practical reference for commissioning proton PBS systems.

**Authors:** Jo, K.; Kim, M. Y.; Jeong, J. H.; Kim, H.; Jeang, E.; Park, S.; Shin, D.; Park, J.; Lim, Y.; Lee, S.

**Venue:** *Journal of the Korean Physical Society*, 67(1), 108-115 (2015).
