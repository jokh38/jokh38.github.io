Proton beam therapy (PBT) is a critical treatment modality for head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC). However, not much is known about drug combinations that may improve the efficacy of PBT. This study tested the feasibility of a three-dimensional tumor-spheroid-based high-throughput screening platform to assess cellular sensitivity against PBT. Spheroids of two HNSCC cell lines (Fadu and Cal27), cultured with Matrigel, were arrayed on a 384-pillar/well plate and exposed to graded doses of protons or targeted drugs, including olaparib, at various concentrations. Proton irradiation produced dose-dependent decreases in cell viability, with Cal27 spheroids demonstrating greater sensitivity than Fadu spheroids. When combined with olaparib, a PARP inhibitor, the treatment showed synergistic effects, particularly in Cal27 cells, while adavosertib showed no additive benefit with protons in either cell line. The 3D pillar/well array platform was a useful tool that provided rapid, quantitative data for evaluating sensitivity to PBT and drug combinations, and combining PBT with olaparib may represent an effective therapeutic strategy for HNSCC patients.
