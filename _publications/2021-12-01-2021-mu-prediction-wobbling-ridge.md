Purpose: This study introduces an output factor (cGy/MU) prediction model for wobbling proton beams over the full range of proton energy, scatterer thickness, and the width of spread-out Bragg peak (SOBP). Methods: From December 2015 to August 2020, 1990 wobbling proton fields were used to treat patients, of which 1714 fields had a diameter smaller than 11 cm and 276 fields had a diameter between 11 and 16 cm. The prediction model was established using polynomial fitting functions, with coefficients determined separately for small and middle wobbling radius cases, covering proton energies between 70 and 230 MeV. Results: The mean output factor difference was 0.22% with a standard deviation of 1.3% for small wobbling radius cases, and the mean value was 0.20% with a standard deviation of 0.79% for middle wobbling radius cases, corresponding to over 93% and 95.5% accuracy respectively. Larger deviations occurred with small field sizes and narrow SOBP widths. Conclusions: A monitor unit prediction model for wobbling proton therapy with ridge filters was successfully developed, with future improvements possible through consideration of field size effects.
